Šoma360 - Privacy Policy
Last updated: 22 August 2026

Šoma360 ("we", "our", "us") provides a cleaning operations app used by
cleaning staff and supervisors to manage shifts, tasks, and site
inspections. This policy explains what information the Šoma360 mobile
and web apps collect, how it's used, and the choices you have.


INFORMATION WE COLLECT

- Account information. Name, username or email, role, and assigned
  site(s), provided by your employer or administrator when your account
  is created.

- Attendance & location data. When you check in or out of a shift or
  scan a site/room QR code, we record the time and, where enabled, your
  device's location to verify you were on site. Location is only
  accessed during these operational actions, not tracked continuously in
  the background.

- Camera & photos. The app uses your camera to scan QR codes and to
  capture photo evidence for tasks, inspections, and incident reports.
  Captured images are uploaded to our servers as part of that record.

- Task & operational records. Task completions, declarations, corrective
  actions, and incident reports you submit through the app.

- Device & usage data. Basic technical data (such as app version and
  device type) used for troubleshooting and reliability.


HOW WE USE THIS INFORMATION

- To operate core features: shift check-ins, task assignment and
  tracking, inspections, and incident reporting.
- To verify attendance and task completion at the correct site.
- To let supervisors and administrators oversee operations at their
  sites.
- To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the app.
- To communicate with you about your account or operational alerts.


WHO CAN SEE YOUR INFORMATION

Your operational data (attendance, tasks, evidence photos, inspections)
is visible to supervisors and administrators at your organisation who
are responsible for the sites you work at. We do not sell your personal
information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own
marketing purposes.


DATA RETENTION

We retain account and operational records for as long as your account is
active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet operational, audit,
and legal record-keeping needs. You can request deletion of your account
data as described below.


YOUR CHOICES

- Camera and location permissions can be managed at any time in your
  device settings. Note that declining these permissions may prevent
  core features (QR scanning, evidence capture, verified check-in) from
  working.
- You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal
  information by contacting us at the email below, or by asking your
  organisation's Šoma360 administrator.


DATA SECURITY

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your
information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) between the app and
our servers and access controls based on your role and assigned
site(s).


CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Šoma360 is a workplace tool intended for use by employed cleaning
staff and supervisors. It is not directed at children, and we do not
knowingly collect information from children.


CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.


CONTACT US

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to
support@smartclean.example.
